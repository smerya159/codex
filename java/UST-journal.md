title: scars of temptation

in a day through the lessons, i was shocked to find the lesson about sex ed...
most of my classmates are laughing, taking things like a joke, which is the best
course to take in talking in a sensitive topic like this. but i know that some
have experience, most do not, some of the experienced were proud, some were ashamed.

despite the jokes and laughter i was feeling ashamed because i felt guilty for
the sins i have commited in my youth. despite the imitations and mockery, theres a
shade of darkness at the back of my head.

i reacted this way because i was the guy that never tells his troubles out loud.
and when i do, it was always hidden through layers of jokes and other 
encryptions to hide what i truly felt because to me, being vulnerable is extremely
hard so this is my own way of being vulnerable.

i beleive that this interaction connects to self awareness...because each of the
jokes must be weighed, too much "joke-y-ness" and ill find myself being 
insensitive or rude, too little, and it might not be read as a joke. having
self awareness also indicates that im aware of who i am and what i do
in situations that i may face. i also have no issues threading through life 
this way so i have no plans to change myself or any aspect of myself for now

i also learnt that, sometimes, sensitive topics can be lightened up by jokes
that everyone knows or relates to, to lighten up the mood. then about my emotions,
i managed to control it by asking myself "if they knew, what would they think?"
and i know very well how looks of utter disgust feel. as for what i would do differently,
i would change nothing. Afterall, everyone had fun, everyone learnt and everyone
enjoyed the lesson despite not being in the planned direction our professor
